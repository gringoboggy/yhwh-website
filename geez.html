The Geʽez & Amharic Bibles

The heart of this project: two standalone Bibles transcribed directly from the original manuscripts — slowly, carefully, witness by witness. This page shows exactly where they stand, and never claims more than the work proves.

New here? Read about the Geʽez Bible reader — the manuscripts and the method — explore the 81 books of the Ethiopian canon, or see why you can copy-paste the text.

The sources we transcribe from

Every reading is taken from real manuscripts and scholarly editions you can see for yourself:

  • Cambridge University Library — MS Add. 1570 — Geʽez Octateuch & Samuel/Kings, in high-resolution IIIF folios.
  • Patrologia Orientalis — printed critical Geʽez editions.
  • The HaCohen Geʽez apocrypha (Wisdom, Sirach) — clean printed text.
